How it works

Four steps

Open your Unreal project, then:

Export

In RGB MoCap: File > Export > FBX.

Add

In SEND IT: ADD FBX, or right-click the file > Send to.

Pick

FIND your MetaHumans and click the ones you want.

Send it

Find the clip under RGBMoCap > Retargeted, with a sequence ready to play.

FAQ

Questions

Where is my licence key after I buy?

In the email from Polar and in your customer portal: sign in with the email you used for the purchase. In SEND IT, click LICENCE, paste the key and click ACTIVATE.

Is there a free trial?

No. SEND IT is a one-time purchase at a low price. Watch the demo videos and check the requirements above before buying.

Can I use it on another computer?

One key works on 2 PCs. To switch, open LICENCE on the old PC and click MOVE TO ANOTHER PC.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for up to 7 days at a time after a successful online check.

How do I get updates?

SEND IT tells you when a new version is out. Download it from your customer portal and unzip it over the old one; your settings, history and licence stay.

Do I need iClone or other paid tools?

No. You need RGB MoCap and Unreal Engine 5. SEND IT does the retarget inside Unreal.
